= feature/conway-suite: rational tangles, Conway notation and the skein relation

Tracks the Conway-suite kernel behind `src/tangle.jl` and `src/skein.jl`:
rational tangles as exact rationals, the Conway calculus, continued fractions
in both directions, 2-bridge classification, and the Conway skein relation
that defines the Alexander-Conway polynomial.

Minimal working prototype: `examples/prototypes/conway-suite.jl`.
Self-contained (standard library only, no KnotKnot import), exits non-zero
unless every pinned assertion holds.

== Conventions pinned by the prototype

The tangle calculus is exact rational arithmetic on `frac(T) in Q`::

* horizontal sum `frac(T + S) = frac(T) + frac(S)`;
* vertical product `frac(T * S) = frac(T) * frac(S)`;
* rotation `frac(rot T) = -1 / frac(T)`;
* mirror `frac(mir T) = -frac(T)`.

Conway notation `[a1, ..., an]` evaluates right-to-left:
`a1 + 1/(a2 + 1/(... + 1/an))`. The empty list is the zero tangle.
`conway_notation` recovers a list by the Euclidean algorithm and round-trips.

The numerator closure of the tangle `p/q` is the 2-bridge link `N(p/q)`: a knot
exactly when `p` is odd, with determinant `|p|`.

2-bridge determinants: `N(3/1) = 3`, `N(5/3) = 5`, `N(5/2) = 5`,
`N(9/2) = 9`. Knot fractions: `3/1`, `5/3`, `5/2`, `9/2` are knots; `2/1` is
the Hopf link (two components, determinant 2).

Tangle identities asserted for several fractions: rotation squared is the
identity, rotation four times is the identity, mirror commutes with rotation,
`T + mirror(T) = 0`, and the sums and products really do add and multiply.

The skein relation, checked on the defining triple::
+
----
grad(3_1) - grad(unknot) = z * grad(Hopf)    1 + z^2 - 1 = z * z
grad(4_1) - grad(3_1)  = z * (-2z)           1 - z^2 - (1 + z^2) = -2z^2
----

Determinant bridge, `|grad(2i)| = |Delta(-1)|`: unknot 1, trefoil 3,
figure-eight 5, cinquefoil 5. Because a knot's Conway polynomial has only even
powers, `(2i)^(2i) = (-4)^i` is real, so this stays exact integer arithmetic.

And the two routes must agree: `N(3/1)` is the trefoil, `N(5/3)` and `N(5/2)`
are both the figure-eight, `N(5/1)` is the cinquefoil. Note that `N(5/3)` and
`N(5/1)` share determinant 5 while being different knots — the determinant is
an obstruction, not a classification.

== Risks to guard against

* **Right-to-left evaluation.** Conway notation is not a standard continued
  fraction read left-to-right; `[1,1,1,1] = 5/3`, not `3/5`. Every helper
  must use the same convention, and the round-trip test is what pins it.
* **The zero tail.** `continued_fraction([a, ..., 0])` has no value (division
  by zero at the last step); the prototype raises rather than returning
  something plausible.
* **Fraction ambiguity.** `conway_notation` returns ONE valid list, not a
  canonical one; the round trip is up to the usual continued-fraction
  ambiguity (`[1,1,1,1]` and `[1,1,2]` give the same `5/3`).
* **Knot vs link fractions.** `is_knot_fraction` looks at the numerator only.
  Even numerators are 2-component links, and the determinant bridge above must
  not be applied to them: a link's Conway polynomial has odd powers and
  `grad(2i)` is then genuinely complex.
* **Smoothing at the diagram level.** The prototype checks the skein relation
  on known triples. `src/skein.jl` computes the triple from a diagram by
  switching and oriented smoothing, which changes the component count; the
  prototype does not reproduce that and should not be mistaken for it.

== Plan going forward

. **Share the exact polynomial type.** The prototype hand-rolls a
   `Dict{Int,Rational{Int}}` Conway polynomial; the production version should
   reuse `LaurentPoly` from `src/laurent.jl` so one polynomial engine serves
   the Alexander, Conway and Jones kernels.
. **Lift the skein relation to diagrams.** Implement `switch_crossing` and
   `smooth_crossing` on PD codes (the smoothing must relabel arcs and track
   components), then assert `verify_conway_skein == 0` on every crossing of
   `3_1` and `4_1` as ULTRAPLAN Phase 4 requires.
. **Skein recursion for the Conway polynomial.** With the diagram-level
   triple in place, compute `conway_polynomial` by recursion on crossing count
   and cross-check it against the Fox-calculus route in
   `feature/alexander-conway`.
. **2-bridge catalogue.** Enumerate `N(p/q)` for `|p|` up to a bound, record
   the knot each one is, and check the determinant/genus/signature against
   `feature/seifert-signature` and the knot table.
. **Rational tangle invariants.** Add numerator and denominator closures with
   their own skein relations, and check the fraction against the determinant
   of the closure diagram.
